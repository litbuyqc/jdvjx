Oopbuy product links / Comparison essentials

What deserves a column?

Use these fields when creating your own list. They make similar-looking options easier to compare.

A practical fashion shortlist template
Field What to record Why it helps
Product & seller Exact product URL, seller name and category. Helps you return to the same listing and avoid confusing similar items.
Selected variant Color, size, style option and any seller-specific variant code. Keeps your notes tied to the option you actually intend to buy.
Measurements Relevant garment dimensions, shoe size guidance or accessory dimensions. Provides a more useful fit comparison than a size letter alone.
Materials & photos Stated composition, close-up images and any unresolved questions. Separates visible details from claims that still need evidence.
Cost estimate Item price, currency, expected fees and delivery estimates where available. Allows you to compare a likely total rather than only the displayed price.
Review status Saved / Needs details / Compared / Ready to review, plus the date checked. Shows what still needs attention before a purchase decision.

Think in delivered cost: item price + applicable domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + payment or conversion charges + applicable taxes and duties. Some components may not apply; a final total requires current quotes for your order and destination.

Direct answers

Before you
add to your list.

A few practical answers for researching Oopbuy spreadsheet finds and comparing fashion listings.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, selected variant, measurements, listed price and review status. This page is a browsing guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use this page to find products?

Choose one of the CAFANS categories near the top of the page. Review the destination listing, then record the exact product link, variant, measurements and price in your own shortlist. Confirm whether that listing can be purchased through your chosen shopping service.

Does a listing photo prove authenticity or quality?

No. A listing photo, brand name or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity or quality. Review seller information, detailed photographs, materials and any available proof of origin before making a decision.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the seller’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest width, length, shoulder width and sleeve length. For shoes, compare the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Is the listed product price the final delivered cost?

Not necessarily. Your delivered cost may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, payment or currency conversion charges and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges with the relevant seller, service and destination authorities.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and shopping research page linking to CAFANS. It does not operate Oopbuy, process orders or represent the brands illustrated on the page.
